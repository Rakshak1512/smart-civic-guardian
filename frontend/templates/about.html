About Smart Civic Guardian

Technology for cleaner, safer and smarter communities.

Our Mission

To make civic issue reporting easier, faster and more transparent through technology.

Our Vision

A connected community where citizens can report problems and authorities can respond efficiently.

Smart Civic Guardian is a technology-driven civic issue management platform designed to connect citizens with authorities through a simple digital reporting system.

1

Citizens can report civic issues using photographs.

2

AI helps identify the issue category.

3

The system suggests priority.

4

Complaints are routed to the appropriate department.

5

Authorities can manage and update complaints.

6

Citizens can track progress until resolution.

Why Smart Civic Guardian?

Real-Time Tracking

Follow your complaint from submission to resolution, at every stage.

AI-Assisted Accuracy

Smart categorization and priority detection reduce delays and misrouted reports.

Direct Accountability

Every report reaches the right department, with no lost paperwork or runaround.

Public Accountability

Dashboards give citizens and authorities a transparent, shared view of progress.
